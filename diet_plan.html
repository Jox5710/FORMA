Meal 1: Power Oats

08:00 AM - Pre-workout

40gPro
75gCarb
15gFat
595Kcal

Ingredients

  • 80g Rolled Oats (dry weight)
  • 1 Scoop Whey Protein (Vanilla)
  • 15g Peanut Butter
  • 100g Blueberries
  • 150ml Almond Milk

Instructions

Cook oats with almond milk and water. Once cooked, let it cool slightly before stirring in whey protein (to prevent clumping). Top with peanut butter and berries.

Meal 2: Chicken & Rice Bowl

01:30 PM - Post-workout

55gPro
90gCarb
10gFat
670Kcal

Ingredients

  • 200g Chicken Breast (raw weight)
  • 120g Basmati Rice (dry weight)
  • 100g Broccoli
  • 1 tsp Olive Oil (for cooking)

Instructions

Dice chicken and season with salt, pepper, and paprika. Pan-fry in olive oil. Steam broccoli. Serve everything over cooked rice. Use zero-calorie hot sauce if desired.

Daily Supplements

  • Creatine Monohydrate 5g Post-workout
  • Omega 3 Fish Oil 2000mg with Meal 1
  • Vitamin D3 + K2 1 Tab with Meal 1

Coach Notes

Weigh all foods RAW/UNCOOKED unless specified otherwise. Consistency is key here. If you need to swap a carb source, ensure the macro totals match the plan. Drink at least 3-4 liters of water daily.
